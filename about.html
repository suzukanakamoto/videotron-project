About Digilight

Professional LED Display Manufacturer & Solution Provider

Company Profile

Leading Indonesia in Display Technology

Digilight is a leading provider of LED videotron modules and digital display solutions in Indonesia, purpose-built to meet the standards of government and state-owned enterprise (BUMN) projects.

With a strong commitment to quality, durability, and compliance with national regulations — including TKDN requirements — Digilight has become a dependable partner for public infrastructure development, from city landmarks and government offices to public monitoring centers and information displays.

Company Culture

Our core values drive our innovation and commitment to excellence.

Our Vision

To be the leading national brand in LED videotron technology, empowering government institutions and state-owned enterprises with reliable, innovative, and locally-supported digital display solutions that support Indonesia's digital transformation.

Our Mission

  • Deliver Quality Products — Provide high-performance, durable, and TKDN-compliant LED videotron modules that meet national technical standards.
  • Support Government Digital Infrastructure — Contribute to the advancement of public communication and information systems through reliable display technology.
  • Provide End-to-End Solutions — Offer complete services from consultation, custom design, and installation to long-term maintenance and technical support.
  • Ensure Compliance & Transparency — Maintain full legal compliance and transparent procurement processes to support government and BUMN partnerships.
  • Build Long-Term Partnerships — Foster trust and collaboration with government institutions and state-owned enterprises through consistent quality and after-sales service.
  • Drive Local Innovation — Continuously develop in-house technical capability and local component integration to reduce dependency on imported technology.

About LongRun LED

Professional LED Display Manufacturer & Solution Provider

Company Profile

Leading the World in Display Technology

Shenzhen Longrun Optoelectronic Co., Ltd, established in 2013, is a professional LED display manufacturer focusing on indoor COB commercial displays and full-scene LED solutions. We integrate R&D, production, sales, and engineering services.

With an independent industrial park covering over 30,000 square meters and a core R&D team with decades of industry experience, LongRun LED is committed to providing global clients with the most reliable and innovative display products.
